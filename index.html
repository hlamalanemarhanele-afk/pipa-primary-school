Historical Background of Pipa Primary School

The school was established in 1938 as an off-shoot of Medingeni primary school. The school offered only Sub A and Sub B. The school was under the Principalship of Mr Sesweni. The school was closed in 1945 because Children were not attending classes as they were forced by parents to head different life stocks.

The school later reopen and relocated to a new site in 1965. The school was under the leadership of Mr Mancha Stephen Mochike until he passed on in 1982. Mr Makgwana Lazarus Motshekga took over the leadership of the school in 1983 until he retired in 2005.

Mr Motshekga was succeeded by Mr Mothoka Ben Machipi in 2006. Mr Matshipi was seconded full time to Professional Educators Union (PEU) in 2010 and that gave Mr Seja Stan Makgalo to act as the Principal until he retired in 2024.

Mrs Matsatsi Jelos Mohale took over as the acting Principal with assistance of Mr Poncho Mabotja. From 8 April 2025, Dr Matshipi M.G leads the school with 41 educators and 1491 learners. No school fees. Quintile 1. Section 21 Yes.

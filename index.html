Historical Background

Pipa Primary School is situated at Maphalle village, ward 24, under Mopani East. Greater Letaba Municipality, Mopani District in Limpopo Province. The school started in 1938 as a farm school. Mr Sesweni (a farm owner) started it with community. It was closed in 1945 because of labour problems.

In 1965 the school was reopened under farm owner Mr P Mochike. Mr Motshekga became first principal until 1989. Mr Matshipi M.G took over from 1990.

In 2006, Mr Matshipi was seconded full time to Professional Educators Union (PEU) in 2010 and that gave Mr Seja Stan Makgalo to act as the Principal until he retired in 2024.

Mrs Matsatsi Jelos Mohale took over as the acting Principal with assistance of Mr Poncho Mabotja. From 8 April 2025, Dr Matshipi M.G leads the school with 41 educators and 1491 learners. No school fees. Quintile 1. Section 21 Yes.

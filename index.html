1938

A school built by community

Pipa began with a shared belief: children in Maphalle deserved access to education.

1938 - The beginning

Established as Pipa Lower Primary School to bring education to Maphalle Village.

1960s - A new name

Renamed in honour of the local community and continued to grow.

1994 - New chapter

Became a no-fee Quintile 1 school ensuring every child can learn.

2026 - Looking ahead

Today 1491 learners Grade R-7 with 41 educators, 25 classrooms.

Learning shaped by a proud legacy.
